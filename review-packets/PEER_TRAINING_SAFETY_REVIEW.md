## Exact behavior under review

- Proposed supporter states: draft, submitted, identity pending, reference pending, training pending, review, approved, suspended, withdrawn, rejected.
- Cornell-authorized identity is required; no “trained” or “vetted” label appears until verifiable gates pass. References use consent-based invitations without required phone numbers.
- Requester/supporter connection is double opt-in. Direct contact information is not revealed; fixed notifications and an in-app/email relay use server IDs. Requesters/supporters can cancel, accept, decline, expire, block, and report as authorized.
- Meetings are limited to appropriate public places; no unsafe late-night framing. Supporters provide informal support/resource navigation only and are not clinicians, investigators, transport, or crisis responders.
- Moderator access is minimized and audited. Report triage, severity, assignment, notes, suspension/reinstatement, incident history, shutdown, and retention are defined in `PEER_SAFETY_OPERATIONS.md`.
